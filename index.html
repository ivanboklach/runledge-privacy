Privacy Policy for the Runledge app for iPhone

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Runledge is a personal running analytics app. It reads your workouts from Apple Health, stores them in your own account, and shows you what they add up to — training load, recovery, sleep quality, race predictions. It is built and run by an individual developer, not a company.

This policy describes exactly what data Runledge handles and what it does with it. It applies to the Runledge iOS app and to the Runledge web app at runledge.vercel.app, which share one account and one database.

Data Runledge collects

Account information. Your email address and password, used only to sign you in. Authentication is handled by Supabase; your password is stored as a cryptographic hash and is never visible to the developer.

Training data you create. Runs and their details — distance, duration, pace, heart rate, elevation, route, perceived effort, notes — along with anything else you enter yourself, such as shoes and gear, planned races, training plans and body weight.

Health and fitness data from Apple Health. Described in the next section.

Runledge contains no advertising SDKs, no third-party analytics, and no crash or usage tracking. It does not read your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera.

Apple Health (HealthKit) data

Runledge asks for permission to read the following from Apple Health. It never writes anything back to Apple Health. Permission is granted by you in the system dialog, per data type, and can be withdrawn at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Runledge.

  • Workouts and workout routes, including the GPS track of a run
  • Heart rate, resting heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Walking and running distance, step count, active energy burned
  • Running dynamics: stride length, vertical oscillation, ground contact time
  • Respiratory rate, blood oxygen and wrist temperature measured during sleep
  • Sleep analysis, including sleep stages

This data is read only while you are using the app — Runledge does not read from Apple Health in the background. It is used for one purpose: to calculate and display your own training and recovery metrics back to you.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.

Health data is never sold, rented or otherwise disclosed to third parties, and is never shared with data brokers, advertising networks or information resellers.

Health data is never used to make decisions about you by anyone other than you — it is not shared with insurers, employers or any similar party.

Where your data is stored

Your account and training data are stored in a private database hosted by Supabase, which acts solely as the hosting provider. Every table is protected by row-level security, so a signed-in account can read only its own rows. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. The app also keeps a copy of your own data on your device so it works offline; that copy is removed when you sign out or delete the app.

Who your data is shared with

Nobody, unless you choose to share it. Runledge has an optional coaching feature: if you invite a coach and accept the link, that coach can see your runs and training metrics. You can end the link at any time, which immediately revokes their access. No other user, and no third party, can see your data.

Your data is not sold, and it is not used to train machine learning models.

How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. To delete your account and everything associated with it, write to the address below from the email address on the account. The account and all of its rows — runs, health metrics, sleep records, gear, plans — are deleted permanently, and this cannot be undone.

You can also remove data at a smaller scale yourself: individual runs can be deleted in the app, and revoking Health permissions stops any further reading from Apple Health.

Children

Runledge is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13.
