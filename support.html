Support

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Runledge is a running analytics app for iPhone. If something is wrong, or you want a feature, write to elitrenthood@gmail.com — it reaches the developer directly. Include your iOS version and what you were doing when it went wrong; that usually saves a round trip.

The app is empty after signing in

Runledge does not record runs itself — it reads what your watch already wrote to Apple Health. Open Runs → Import and allow access when iOS asks. If you declined earlier, grant it again in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Runledge, then import.

Some runs are missing

Only workouts recorded as runs are imported. A walk, a hike or a workout recorded by an app that never wrote to Apple Health will not appear.

Recovery or sleep shows nothing

Those need overnight data — heart rate variability, resting heart rate, breathing rate and sleep stages — which means wearing the watch to bed. Expect a couple of weeks before the baseline is meaningful; the score compares each night against your own normal, and it has to learn what that is.

A number here disagrees with another app

Most likely your heart rate zones. Runledge derives training load from time in zone, so its zones have to match how you actually train. Set them under Profile → HR Zones.

Deleting your account

Profile → Delete Account, confirmed with your password. Everything — runs, health records, gear, races and plans — is deleted permanently and cannot be recovered. See the privacy policy for what is stored in the first place.

Using it on the web

The same account works at runledge.vercel.app; both clients read the same data.
